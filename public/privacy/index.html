Rapid Log

Privacy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Rapid Log is a daily log. It keeps what you write and the account you signed in with, and nothing else. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising anywhere in it.

What is collected

Only two things.

  • Your Google account details, if you sign in — your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. These come from Google when you choose to sign in and are held by Firebase Authentication.
  • The entries you write — their text, whether each is a task, event or note, which section of the day it belongs to, any start or end time you set, whether you marked it a priority, and when it was created. These are stored in Cloud Firestore.

That is the whole list. Nothing is inferred about you, and nothing is collected in the background.

Using it as a guest

If you continue as a guest, nothing leaves your browser. Entries live in the page's memory and are gone when you close it. They are never sent anywhere.

One exception, and only if you ask for it: choosing to sign in while you have unsaved guest entries stores them briefly in your browser's own local storage, so they survive the trip to Google and back. They are cleared the moment they are saved to your account, and expire by themselves after ten minutes.

What Rapid Log does not do

  • No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no profiling or targeting.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • No third-party services beyond Google, described below.
  • No cookies are set for tracking.

Where it is stored

On Google Cloud, through Firebase — Firebase Authentication for the account, Cloud Firestore for the entries, and Firebase Hosting to serve the app. Google processes this data on Rapid Log's behalf under its own terms.

Access rules on the database name the owner of every entry, so an entry can only be read or changed by the account that created it. Those rules are public and can be read in the source code.

Firebase Hosting keeps ordinary web server logs — IP address, browser and time of request — as any web server does.

Signing in with Google

Signing in asks Google only for your basic profile: name, email address and picture. Rapid Log cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or anything else in your Google account, and never asks to.

You can withdraw that access at any time from your Google account permissions.

Your data, your call

  • Delete any entry from inside the app; deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Withdraw Rapid Log's access to your Google account at the link above.
  • Ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted, by email — see below.

Children

Rapid Log is not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Rapid Log is open source, so every revision is visible in its public history.
