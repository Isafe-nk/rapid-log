Rapid Log

Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Rapid Log is a daily log. It keeps what you write and the account you signed in with, and nothing else. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising anywhere in it.

What is collected

Only two things.

  • Your Google account details, if you sign in — your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. These come from Google when you choose to sign in and are held by Firebase Authentication.
  • The entries you write — their text, whether each is a task, event or note, which section of the day it belongs to, any start or end time you set, whether you marked it a priority, and when it was created. These are stored in Cloud Firestore.

That is the whole list. Nothing is inferred about you, and nothing is collected in the background.

Using it as a guest

If you continue as a guest, nothing leaves your browser. Entries live in the page's memory and are gone when you close it. They are never sent anywhere.

One exception, and only if you ask for it: choosing to sign in while you have unsaved guest entries stores them briefly in your browser's own local storage, so they survive the trip to Google and back. They are cleared the moment they are saved to your account. If you change your mind and never finish signing in, they are older than ten minutes by the next time you open Rapid Log, and are discarded then. They never leave your browser either way.

What Rapid Log does not do

  • No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no profiling or targeting.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • No third-party services beyond Google, described below.
  • No cookies are set for tracking.

Where it is stored

On Google Cloud, through Firebase — Firebase Authentication for the account, Cloud Firestore for the entries, and Firebase Hosting to serve the app. Google processes this data on Rapid Log's behalf under its own terms.

Access rules on the database name the owner of every entry, so an entry can only be read or changed by the account that created it. Those rules are public and can be read in the source code.

Firebase Hosting keeps ordinary web server logs — IP address, browser and time of request — as any web server does.

Where in the world

The database region is nam5, a multi-region in the United States. If you use Rapid Log from anywhere else, your entries are stored in the United States and cross borders to get there.

Google operates that infrastructure and is the only processor involved. Nothing is passed to anyone beyond Google's own services listed above.

How long it is kept

  • Entries are kept until you delete them. Nothing expires and nothing is removed on a schedule. An entry written years ago is still there unless you removed it.
  • The app only loads the last 30 days at a time, and reaches further back when you open an older date. That is a loading window, not a deletion policy — older entries are untouched.
  • Your account details are kept while the account exists. Ask for it to be deleted and the entries go with it.
  • Guest entries are never stored at all. The one exception, the sign-in hand-off described above, clears itself within ten minutes.

How it is protected

  • Everything travels over HTTPS. The app refuses to load any other way.
  • Rapid Log never sees your password. Signing in happens on Google's own pages; the app only ever receives a token saying who you are.
  • The database denies everything by default and allows a read or a write only when the signed-in account owns that entry. The rules are checked on Google's servers, not in the app, so they hold even if the app is modified. They are published in full in the source code.
  • No copy of your log is kept anywhere else — no backups of our own, no exports, no analytics store.

No system is perfectly secure, and Rapid Log is a personal project rather than an audited service. Treat it accordingly: it is a daily log, not a vault for anything sensitive.

Your rights over this data

  • See it — your whole log is visible in the app; nothing is held back from you.
  • Correct it — any entry can be edited in place.
  • Delete it — any entry, immediately and permanently, or the whole account by email.
  • Withdraw access — revoke Rapid Log from your Google account at any time, at the link below.
  • Take it elsewhere — ask by email and you will be sent your entries as a file.

Signing in with Google

Signing in asks Google only for your basic profile: name, email address and picture. Rapid Log cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or anything else in your Google account, and never asks to.

You can withdraw that access at any time from your Google account permissions.

Children

Rapid Log is not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Rapid Log is open source, so every revision is visible in its public history.
